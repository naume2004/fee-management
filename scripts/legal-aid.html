Legal Aid Services

Information on legal rights, access to justice, and legal assistance available to inmates and their families.

⚖️  Legal Aid is provided free of charge to qualifying inmates. Contact Uganda Legal Aid Services at 0800 200 500 (toll-free).

Rights of Inmates

  • Right to be informed of the charges against you.
  • Right to access legal representation at every stage of proceedings.
  • Right to humane treatment and medical care.
  • Right to communicate with family members or next of kin.
  • Right to receive visits from a legal representative in private.
  • Right to appeal a conviction or sentence.
  • Right to petition the court for bail or bond.

How to Apply for Legal Aid

  • Speak to the Officer-in-Charge at your facility and request legal aid.
  • A paralegal or lawyer from Uganda Legal Aid Services will be assigned.
  • Family members can also apply on behalf of an inmate by visiting any district legal aid office.
  • Applications are assessed based on financial need and nature of the case.

Available Legal Aid Organisations

  • Uganda Legal Aid Services (ULAS) — Kampala, 0800 200 500
  • Justice Defenders (formerly African Prisons Project) — luzira.justicedefenders.org
  • Legal Aid Service Providers Network (LASPNET) — Multiple district offices
  • Uganda Human Rights Commission — 0414-348-007
  • Foundation for Human Rights Initiative (FHRI) — 0414-510-498

Filing a Complaint

If your rights have been violated while in custody, you may file a complaint with:

  • The Officer-in-Charge of the prison facility.
  • The Uganda Human Rights Commission.
  • The Inspector General of Government (IGG).
  • The Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions (ODPP).
